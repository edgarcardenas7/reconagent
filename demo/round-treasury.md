# ReconAgent For Round Treasury

## Problem

Treasury teams need controlled workflows for payment approvals, duplicate payment checks, and
auditability.

## Demo

Use the sample Beta BV duplicate payment. ReconAgent detects duplicate-sized payments inside seven
days and routes the action to `hold_for_review`.

## Talking Point

"For Round, I would position this as a deterministic treasury workflow runner where AI explains
exceptions but approvals and audit remain controlled."
